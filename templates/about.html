Why Tazaj exists

Every day, bakeries, cafés, restaurants, and grocers end the day with fresh food they can't sell tomorrow. At the same time, people are looking for affordable, quality meals near home or work.

Tazaj bridges that gap—keeping good food in circulation, supporting neighborhood businesses, and making sustainable habits effortless.
