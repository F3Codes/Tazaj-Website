Quick answers

Common questions about surplus orders and pickup windows.

What is included in a surplus order?

Items depend on what each store has left over that day—such as pastries, meals, salads, or produce—always priced well below retail.

Is the food fresh?

Yes. Stores list unsold food from that day that is still fresh and good to eat, rather than letting it go to waste.

Why are pickup windows time-limited?

Pickup windows match when the store wraps up service so staff can hand orders over smoothly.
